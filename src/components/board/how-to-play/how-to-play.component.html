How to play {{ data.variant.label }} Squares

  1. Add your players and give each one a color.
  2. Spin the spinner.
  3. Solve the math problem to get your answer.
  4. Find a square on the board with that answer and draw a line on one of its sides.
  5. Draw the last side of a square and you capture it.
  6. No match on the board? Skip your turn.
  7. Play until the board fills up. Most squares wins.
