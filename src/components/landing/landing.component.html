Why it works in class

trending_up

The math is the game

Students are solving math facts on every turn. They're just too busy trying to win to notice.

psychology

There's real strategy

Knowing the answer is only part of it. Players also have to decide which square to take, what to block, and whether to play it safe or go for it.

bolt

Open it and play

Works in any browser. Add your players, pick a game, and you're playing in under a minute.

groups

Flexible for any class format

2 to 4 players. Works for partner work, small groups, or a few spare minutes at the end of class. Each game has multiple levels.

How to play

  1. Choose a game and a level.
  2. Add your players and give each one a color.
  3. Spin the spinner.
  4. Find a square on the board with your answer and draw a line on one of its sides.
  5. Draw the last side of a square and you capture it.
  6. No match? Skip your turn.
  7. Play until the board fills up. Most squares wins.
